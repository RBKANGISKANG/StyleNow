One paying business brings about €1,425.60 a year.

Booking value = everything customers pay through StyleNow; most of it goes to the businesses. StyleNow income = only what stays with StyleNow after paying the businesses (commission + Prime share, no-show fees, unused vouchers). Gross profit = StyleNow income − card fees on StyleNow’s own share (the businesses pay the card fee on theirs). Profit = gross profit − costs. How it is calculated: businesses pay no commission in their first 12 months, so a year’s paying base is the businesses live at the end of the year before; newcomers still bring Prime, no-show and voucher income. Value = run-rate × multiple. Marketplaces usually sell or raise at about 3–8× their own income (not the booking value), depending on growth and profit. Taxes, churn and financing are not included — check with an accountant or investor.
